# 📉 Fit.ly Subscriber Retention Analysis

End-to-end customer churn analysis using **SQL** and **Python** to identify the primary drivers of subscriber attrition and recommend data-driven retention strategies.

This project analyzes customer demographics, subscription plans, customer support interactions, and service usage to uncover factors influencing churn and support business decision-making.

---

# 🎯 Project Objective

Fit.ly seeks to reduce customer churn by understanding why subscribers cancel their memberships.

The objective of this project was to:

- Identify the strongest predictors of customer churn
- Analyze customer behavior across subscription segments
- Quantify churn patterns using SQL and Python
- Deliver actionable recommendations to improve customer retention

---

# 💼 Business Problem

Customer acquisition is significantly more expensive than customer retention.

Without understanding why customers leave, retention initiatives become reactive rather than proactive.

This analysis helps business stakeholders prioritize retention efforts by identifying high-risk customer segments and the operational factors most strongly associated with churn.

---

# 📂 Dataset

The analysis combines multiple customer datasets, including:

- Customer Profiles
- Subscription Information
- Customer Support Records
- Service Usage Metrics

Key variables include:

- Customer demographics

- Subscription plan
- Support ticket activity
- Resolution times

- Churn status

# 💡 Key Insights

- Customers with longer support resolution times exhibited significantly higher churn rates.
- Month-to-month subscribers were considerably more likely to cancel than customers on long-term contracts.
- Customer tenure strongly influenced retention, with newer customers showing the highest churn risk.
- Customer support interactions provided early indicators of potential subscriber loss.
- Combining customer profile, subscription, and support data enabled more targeted retention strategies.

---

# 📈 Business Recommendations

- Prioritize rapid resolution of customer support tickets.
- Develop targeted retention campaigns for high-risk subscriber segments.
- Encourage migration from monthly to long-term subscription plans.
- Implement proactive monitoring for customers exhibiting early churn indicators.
- Track customer health metrics through automated retention dashboards.
